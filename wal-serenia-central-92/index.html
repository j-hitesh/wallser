THE PROJECT

A Vibrant High-Street Destination
in the Heart of Sector 92

WAL Serenia Central 92 is a planned commercial retail destination in Sector 92, New Gurgaon, positioned directly in front of Serenia Tower 1 and Tower 2 and immediately adjoining DLF Garden City. Designed to provide essential retail, dining, and boutique commercial services, the development features 53 commercial units comprising 52 high-street retail shops and 1 expansive double-height anchor showroom (Shop 53).

The commercial block is thoughtfully planned with generous 4.05 m floor-to-floor clear heights on both Ground Floor and First Floor / Mezzanine levels, offering a total saleable area of 32,973.27 sq.ft (3,063.32 sq.m) and total carpet area of 16,486.64 sq.ft (1,531.66 sq.m). Strategically set along a 12-meter wide dedicated service road and a 60-meter wide sector road, it features dedicated pedestrian pathways, organized vehicle parking, fire-tender circulation, public toilets, and drinking water facilities.

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is WAL Serenia Central 92 located?

WAL Serenia Central 92 is located in Sector 92, New Gurgaon, directly adjacent to WAL Serenia 92 and DLF Garden City, fronting prime sector arterial roads.

What types of commercial spaces are available?

The development offers 52 high-street retail shops (ranging from 242 to 293 sq.ft carpet / 484 to 586 sq.ft saleable) and 1 double-height corner anchor showroom (2,278 sq.ft carpet / 4,557 sq.ft saleable).

What is the residential catchment in Sector 92?

The project enjoys an immediate captive audience of over 20,000 affluent resident families across premium high-rise condominiums in Sector 92 and adjacent Sectors 91 and 93.

What is the building structure and height configuration?

WAL Serenia Central 92 features 53 commercial units planned across Ground and First Floor / Mezzanine levels with generous 4.05 m floor-to-floor heights, fronted by a 12-meter dedicated service road.

Is the project registered with HARERA?

Yes, WAL Serenia Central 92 is developed in strict accordance with the Haryana Real Estate Regulatory Authority (HARERA Gurugram) guidelines and statutory approvals.

How is Sector 92 connected to Delhi and Gurgaon commercial hubs?

The project offers rapid access to Pataudi Road (500m), Dwarka Expressway (3.5 km), NH-48 (6.5 km), and IMT Manesar (5.0 km), with seamless signal-free transit to IGI Airport.

What amenities are provided for shoppers and business owners?

Facilities include dedicated drop-off zones, high-speed passenger elevators, multi-level visitor parking, 100% power backup, 24×7 security with CCTV, and landscaped open shopping plazas.

Can restaurants and cafes operate at WAL Serenia Central 92?

Yes, designated spaces on ground and upper terraces are provisioned with requisite ventilation and outdoor seating areas suited for dining outlets, bakeries, and cafes.

Why is high-street commercial in New Gurgaon an attractive investment?

High-street retail units benefit from daily neighborhood necessity footfall, lower maintenance costs than enclosed malls, and superior rental yields driven by rapid residential occupancy.

How can I request pricing details and available shop inventory?

You can request the latest commercial price list, floor layouts, and payment plans by filling out the enquiry form or contacting the official sales team.
