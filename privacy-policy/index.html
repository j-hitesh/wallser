01

Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the official web portals, mobile experiences, digital communication channels, and sales inquiries managed by WAL Developments Pvt. Ltd. ("WAL Developments", "Company", "we", "us", or "our"), including the website located at www.walprojects.com.

WAL Developments is committed to honoring your right to data privacy. We recognize that prospective homebuyers, investors, channel partners, and visitors entrust us with valuable personal information when inquiring about our residential, commercial, and mixed-use real estate projects in Gurgaon. This document explains how we collect, store, safeguard, disclose, and handle that information in accordance with applicable laws in India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

✦ Commitment to Transparency

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing brokers. Any information provided to WAL Developments is utilized solely to facilitate your property search, consultation, site visits, and transactional requirements.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, sales lounges, and digital campaigns, we may collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal Identifiable Information (Provided Voluntarily)

  • Contact Information: Full name, telephone/mobile number, email address, postal address, and city of residence.
  • Property Preferences: Specific projects of interest (e.g., WAL Serenia 92, WAL Street 73, WAL Street 88A, WAL Serenia Central 92), unit configuration preferences (e.g., 3 BHK residences, SCO plots, retail shops), budget range, intended purchase timeline, and purpose of investment.
  • Inquiry Context: Inquiries submitted through our forms, requests for digital brochures, schedule for physical site visits, callback requests, and chat transcripts.
  • Financial & Identity Verification (At Booking): If you proceed with a property application, government-issued identifiers (PAN card, Aadhaar card, passport details for NRIs), banking details, and source of funds documentation required by statutory authorities and HARERA.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Device & Connection Data: Internet Protocol (IP) address, operating system, browser type and version, screen resolution, device brand and model, language preferences, and internet service provider (ISP).
  • Website Usage Patterns: Pages visited, duration of stay, click streams, referring website URLs, outbound link clicks, date and time stamps, and bounce rates.
  • Geolocation Data: Broad geographic location derived from IP address (such as city, state, and country level) to ensure relevant project offerings.
03

Methods of Data Collection

We gather information through structured and secure touchpoints:

  1. Website Lead Forms: When you complete the "Enquire Now", "Request a Callback", "Download Brochure", or "Schedule Site Visit" forms on our website.
  2. Direct Communications: When you contact our sales team via phone calls (+91-9873461828), official WhatsApp conversations, email correspondences, or SMS.
  3. Experience Centres & Site Visits: When you physically visit the WAL Developments sales gallery or project sites (Next to DLF Garden City, Sector 92, Gurgaon) and complete visitor log entries.
  4. Cookies and Web Beacons: Small data files placed on your device to maintain session integrity, analyze web traffic, and personalize your experience.
  5. Authorized Channel Partners & RERA Brokers: Authorized channel partners registered under Haryana RERA who submit qualified customer inquiries on your express instruction.
04

Purpose & Use of Your Information

WAL Developments processes your personal data strictly for legitimate business, transactional, and compliance purposes:

  • Personalized Property Consultation: Responding to inquiries with accurate pricing, floor plans, cost sheets, payment schedules, and inventory availability.
  • Coordinating Site Visits: Arranging guided site walkthroughs, model residence inspections, and appointments with project relationship managers.
  • Home Loan & Financial Coordination: With your explicit consent, connecting you with approved banking partners and NBFCs for home loan pre-approvals and subsidy schemes.
  • Transactional Fulfillment: Processing application forms, issuing provisional booking receipts, executing allotment agreements, and registering documents with Haryana RERA.
  • Service & Project Updates: Sharing construction milestone reports, architectural developments, regulatory approvals, and festive launch announcements.
  • Website Security & Analytics: Preventing spam submissions, detecting fraudulent activity, auditing web traffic, and refining user interface performance.
  • Statutory Compliance: Fulfilling mandatory reporting obligations under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA), the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), and tax regulations.
05

Consent & Legal Grounds for Processing

In alignment with Section 6 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Informed, Explicit Consent: When you tick the consent box or submit a lead generation form containing the declaration "By submitting, you agree to be contacted about your property enquiry", you give free, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent to be contacted by our sales advisors.
  • Contractual Necessity: Processing necessary for the performance of a property booking application, allotment letter, or Builder-Buyer Agreement to which you are a party.
  • Legal Obligation: Processing necessary to comply with applicable statutory laws, court orders, or directives from the Real Estate Regulatory Authority (HARERA).
  • Legitimate Uses: Safeguarding against security breaches, fraud prevention, and operational network stability.
06

Your Privacy Rights under Indian Law

Under the DPDP Act 2023 and Indian data protection principles, you are a "Data Principal" and hold specific enforceable rights regarding your personal data:

Your Right What It Means How to Exercise
Right to Access You can request a summary of the personal data held by us, identities of processors, and purposes of processing. Email us at privacy@walprojects.com
Right to Correction You have the right to correct inaccurate or outdated contact numbers, emails, or postal addresses. Contact your assigned relationship manager or our grievance officer.
Right to Erasure You may request deletion of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the original inquiry purpose. Send a written erasure request to our Privacy Desk.
Right to Withdraw Consent You can opt out of promotional messages, phone calls, or newsletters at any time without penalty. Reply "STOP" to SMS/WhatsApp or click "Unsubscribe" in emails.
Right to Grievance Redressal You can register a formal complaint regarding data handling before our designated Grievance Officer. File a written complaint via grievance@walprojects.com
Right to Nominate You have the right to nominate another individual to exercise privacy rights in the event of death or incapacity. Submit a verified nomination letter to our corporate office.
07

Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

WAL Developments enforces strict confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information. Disclosures are conducted strictly on a "need-to-know" basis under enforceable contractual agreements:

  • Authorized Service Providers: Trusted third-party technology providers who operate our Customer Relationship Management (CRM) databases, cloud servers, transactional SMS gateways, and secure email dispatch platforms. All providers are bound by strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).
  • Financial Institutions & Banks: Recognized banking institutions (e.g., SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis Bank) only when you explicitly request assistance in processing a home loan application.
  • Haryana Real Estate Regulatory Authority (HARERA): Project booking registers, legal filings, and mandatory quarterly updates as required by the Haryana Real Estate Regulatory Authority.
  • Legal & Law Enforcement Authorities: When compelled by valid subpoenas, court orders, or governmental search warrants under Indian legislation.
  • Corporate Restructuring: In the event of a merger, joint venture, asset acquisition, or corporate reorganization, customer records may be transferred subject to the same privacy commitments.
08

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies, web beacons, and related tracking scripts to deliver a seamless browsing experience. A cookie is a small alphanumeric text file stored on your computer or mobile device.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the fundamental operation of the website, including form submissions, security validation, and page routing.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Used by analytical tools (such as Google Analytics) to aggregate anonymous statistics regarding visitor counts, dwell time, and site navigation patterns.
  • Marketing & Retargeting Cookies: Used to display relevant property advertisements when you browse other websites across the web. These cookies remember your interest in specific developments (e.g., WAL Serenia 92).

You can control or disable cookies through your web browser settings at any time (e.g., Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox). Disabling certain cookies may impact form responsiveness or personalized features.

09

Data Security & Storage Infrastructure

WAL Developments implements enterprise-grade technical, administrative, and physical safeguards to prevent unauthorized data access, accidental loss, alteration, or unlawful processing:

  • SSL/TLS 256-Bit Encryption: All communications between your browser and our servers are encrypted via Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure (HTTPS).
  • Data Localization in India: In accordance with DPDP Act recommendations, primary customer records and server infrastructure reside in secure data centers located within India.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Access to customer personal data is strictly restricted to authorized sales executives, compliance attorneys, and CRM managers with unique authentication credentials.
  • Periodic Security Audits: Regular vulnerability scans, firewall protections, and database backups to ensure operational resilience.
10

Data Retention Policy

We retain your personal information only for the duration necessary to satisfy the purposes for which it was gathered:

  • Inquiry Leads: Inactive property inquiries where no commercial transaction occurs are retained for up to 36 months to facilitate future inquiries, after which records are anonymized or securely deleted.
  • Active Buyers & Allottees: Records relating to verified property bookings, payments, and registered deeds are retained permanently or for statutory retention periods mandated by HARERA, the Income Tax Act, 1961, and the Companies Act, 2013.
  • Regulatory & Dispute Records: Retained for the statutory limitation period required to defend or prosecute potential legal claims.
11

Protection of Minors & Children

Our website and real estate offerings are strictly intended for individuals who have attained 18 years of age and hold legal capacity to enter into binding contracts under the Indian Contract Act, 1872. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from minors. If we discover that personal data of a minor has been collected without parental consent, we will take immediate measures to purge such information from our records.
