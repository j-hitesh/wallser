04

Area Measurements: Carpet Area vs Saleable / Super Area

WAL Developments maintains complete clarity regarding real estate measurement metrics in compliance with Indian legislation:

  • Statutory Carpet Area: The legally binding metric for all residential apartment sales is the Carpet Area, defined under Section 2(k) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 as "the net usable floor area of an apartment, excluding the area covered by the external walls, areas under services shafts, exclusive balcony or verandah area and exclusive open terrace area, but includes the area covered by the internal partition walls of the apartment."
  • Saleable Area / Super Area: Any references to "Saleable Area" (e.g., 1,250 Sq. Ft. or 1,350 Sq. Ft. at WAL Serenia 92) or plot dimensions in square yards (e.g., 115–298 Sq. Yd. at WAL Street 73) are provided as conventional market benchmarks. The definitive schedule of Carpet Area, Exclusive Balcony Area, and Pro-rata Common Area is detailed exclusively in the statutory Allotment Agreement.

Indicative Pricing, Payment Schedules & Statutory Taxes

Any pricing, rate cards, promotional launch estimates, or payment schedules (such as the published 25:25:25:25* construction-linked plan) displayed on this website are subject to specific terms:

  • Starting Estimates: Published starting prices (e.g., "₹1.47 Cr* onwards") indicate the base selling price for base configuration units at initial release and are subject to dynamic revision without prior written notice.
  • Exclusions from Base Price: Unless explicitly written in the cost sheet, the base price does not include:
    • Goods and Services Tax (GST) as levied by the Government of India.
    • Stamp Duty, Registration Charges, and legal documentation fees.
    • Interest-Free Maintenance Security (IFMS) and sinking fund contributions.
    • Electricity meter connection, water connection, and dual-meter infrastructure fees.
    • Preferential Location Charges (PLC) for park-facing, corner, or higher-floor units.
    • Club membership charges or external development charges (EDC/IDC) where applicable.
  • Payment Milestones: Installment due dates and linked construction stages are governed strictly by the written payment schedule annexed to your formal booking agreement.
