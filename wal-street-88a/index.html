THE PROJECT

A Premier Commercial Address
at the CPR & Expressway Junction

WAL Street 88A is an exclusive plotted commercial Shop-Cum-Office (SCO) development strategically located in Sector 88A, Gurugram. Fronting the arterial commercial corridor connecting Pataudi Road, Central Peripheral Road (CPR), and Dwarka Expressway (NPR), the project delivers maximum commercial exposure with dual-road accessibility.

Featuring an organized plotted layout of 27 premium SCO plots (numbered A1 through A27), each with a standardized 137 sq. yd. footprint, WAL Street 88A is planned with a proposed front service road, 24m wide sector road, and dedicated parking bays. With permissible Basement + Ground + 3 Upper Floors, it offers unmatched autonomy for retail flagship stores, financial institutions, corporate offices, and fine-dining restaurants.

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is WAL Street 88A located?

WAL Street 88A is located in Sector 88A, Gurugram, directly along the strategic arterial corridor connecting Pataudi Road, Dwarka Expressway (NPR), and Central Peripheral Road (CPR).

What property type is offered at WAL Street 88A?

The project offers premium plotted commercial Shop-Cum-Office (SCO) plots with freehold-style ownership and individual floor leasing autonomy.

How many SCO plots are available at WAL Street 88A?

The master plan features a boutique collection of 27 exclusive commercial SCO plots, numbered sequentially from A1 to A27.

What is the plot size at WAL Street 88A?

Each commercial SCO plot features a standardized footprint of 137 sq. yd., designed for efficient multi-level commercial operations.

What is the permissible building height and configuration?

Each plot allows construction of Basement + Ground + 3 Upper Floors (B + G + 3), subject to standard building bylaws and approved architectural plans.

What road infrastructure and access are provided?

The development features a Proposed Front Service Road directly in front of the plots, a 24-metre wide sector road on the western edge, and 5-metre and 4-metre internal peripheral roads.

What parking and utility facilities are planned?

Dedicated surface visitor parking bays are provided along the front service road and peripheral circulation lanes, complemented by a designated on-site Utility Block and 100% power backup provisions.

Is WAL Street 88A registered with HARERA?

Yes, WAL Street 88A is registered with the Haryana Real Estate Regulatory Authority (HARERA Gurugram). Full project filings and approvals can be verified on haryanarera.gov.in.

What are the key connectivity routes from the project?

The project provides direct access to Pataudi Road, 1.5 km from Dwarka Expressway, 2.0 km from Central Peripheral Road (CPR), and swift signal-free transit to NH-48 and IGI Airport.

How can I request the official price list and payment plan?

You can request the latest cost sheet, available plot facing details, and customized payment schedules by submitting the enquiry form or scheduling an on-site consultation.
