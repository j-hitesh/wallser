THE PROJECT

A Commercial Address
Made for Visibility

WAL Street 73 is a premier plotted commercial Shop-Cum-Office (SCO) development situated prominently on an 84*m wide road on SPR in Sector 73, Gurugram *(60m road + 24m service road with 30m green belt). Design inspired by Federal Hall on Wall Street, New York, with Romanesque Pillars, a Royal Façade, and Ethereal Architecture, it delivers a grand and majestic landmark designed to offer a unique Work and Leisure experience.

Spread across approximately 2.825 acres of prime commercial land, WAL Street 73 offers flexible SCO plot sizes ranging from 115 to 298 sq. yd. With a defined uniform façade and the freedom of constructing a basement + double-height ground floor + 3 upper floors, all plots are two-side open with vehicle-free boulevard circulation, a central landscaped courtyard featuring air filters, a mini amphitheatre, LED screen, waterbody, and trellis pergola.
