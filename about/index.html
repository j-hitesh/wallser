Buying a home or plot is a significant decision. Find clear answers about WAL projects, site visits, booking and the buying process.

↗

A conversation can help.

Site visits, price breakdowns and home-loan assistance, all in one call.

01Are WAL projects RERA registered?+

Yes, WAL projects are registered under Haryana RERA. Registration details can be verified on the Haryana RERA portal before making a booking.

02What is the typical booking amount?+

The booking amount can vary depending on the project, unit type and payment plan. Our team can provide the latest booking amount and payment schedule for your shortlisted property.

03Do you assist with home loans?+

Yes. Our sales team can help connect you with available home-loan options from banks and financial institutions and guide you through the documentation process.

04Can I schedule a physical site visit?+

Yes. Request a site visit through the enquiry form. Our team will coordinate the visit based on your preferred date, time and project availability.

05Where can I find WAL project information?+

Explore our residential and commercial project pages for locations, configurations and project details. Contact our team for any further information before you make a decision.

06How can I get the latest price and availability?+

Project prices, inventory and offers can change. Submit an enquiry and our team will share the latest available information for your preferred project and configuration.
