The Project

A New Address for
Wellness-Led Living

WAL Serenia 92 is a residential development by WAL Developments in Sector 92, Gurgaon, next to DLF Garden City. Planned around wellness, community living and everyday comfort, it combines high-rise homes with green areas, social spaces and recreation.

The project is planned in two phases, with Phase 1 of approximately 4.073 acres and Phase 2 of 0.502 acres. The published project information includes two residential towers, 416 residential units and 3 BHK homes in 1,250 and 1,350 sq. ft. configurations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is WAL Serenia 92 located?

WAL Serenia 92 is located in Sector 92, New Gurgaon, next to DLF Garden City.

What apartment configurations are available?

The published project page lists 3 BHK wellness residences in 1,250 and 1,350 sq. ft. saleable area options.

What is the starting price of WAL Serenia 92?

Published indicative prices start at ₹1.47 Cr* for 1,250 sq. ft. and ₹1.59 Cr* for 1,350 sq. ft. homes. Prices and charges can change.

What is the payment plan?

The displayed payment plan is 25:25:25:25*. Request the current written payment schedule and applicable terms.

What amenities are planned at Serenia 92?

A swimming pool, fitness centre, sports courts, yoga and meditation areas, gardens, clubhouse, children’s play spaces, senior-friendly pathways and healthcare-oriented amenities are listed.

What is the RERA registration number?

The project page lists RERA-GRG-PROJ-2249-2026.

Is there an early-buyer launch offer?

Yes. The published offer mentions a ₹11,750/sq. ft. launch rate and selected benefits for the first 100 buyers, subject to availability and applicable terms.

Is WAL Serenia 92 IGBC certified?

WAL states that the project is IGBC Green Homes Platinum precertified. This is precertification, not a claim of final completed-building certification.
